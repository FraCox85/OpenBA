# BACCM Canvas Template

The BACCM (Business Analysis Core Concept Model) defines six concepts that are always present in BA work. Use this canvas to ensure every analysis and artifact is grounded in all six.

---

## Canvas Format

```md
## BACCM Canvas — [Subject]

### Need / Bisogno
What business problem or opportunity exists?
_TBD_

### Change / Cambiamento
What change is required to address the need?
_TBD_

### Solution / Soluzione
What type of solution could address the need? (Describe direction, not implementation)
_TBD_

### Stakeholder / Parti Interessate
Who is affected by, benefits from, or has authority over the change?

| Stakeholder | Role | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| TBD | TBD | TBD |

### Value / Valore
What measurable value would the change create? For whom?
_TBD_

### Context / Contesto
What constraints, regulations, organizational culture, systems, or environmental factors apply?
_TBD_
```

---

## Usage Guidance

- **During Situation Analysis**: Fill the canvas at project level to ground the entire analysis.
- **Per Need**: Fill a lightweight version to validate that the need is real and complete.
- **Per Requirement**: Use as a sanity check — does this requirement still connect to all six concepts?
- **Per PBI**: Use as a quick "why does this exist?" validation during grooming.

The canvas is not a form to fill robotically. It is a thinking tool. If a concept has no answer, that's a signal — either the analysis is incomplete, or the artifact is disconnected from business value.
